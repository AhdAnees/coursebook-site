Questions

Is it free?

For students, yes: joining a course and your notebook are free. Courses are set up for institutions and instructors; during the beta, contact support@liyaanote.app.

What does "beta" mean?

Liyaa Note is in its test phase with its first courses. Everything works, and we're improving it from your feedback. Tell us what you think at support@liyaanote.app.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Liyaa Note is Android-only for now. An iPhone version is planned.

Why does Android warn me when installing?

Liyaa Note isn't on Google Play yet, so Android asks you to allow the install. The install guide shows exactly what to tap. Once it's on Google Play this step goes away.

Who can see my notes?

Only you. Your notebook stays on your phone, with an optional private backup. What you share in a course is seen only by that course's members. See the privacy policy.

What if I change phones?

Sign in on the new phone: your courses, chats and Memories are there, and your notes come back from your backup. Recordings and files can be moved with a backup file saved to your own Google Drive or OneDrive.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile & Settings → Delete my account. Or see how to delete your account.
