Running a course? Ask us to start one.

Liyaa Note is for students and trainees, the trainers who teach them, and classes that want to stay in touch. Trainers: write to support@liyaanote.app with your name and your course, and we'll switch on course creation for your account.

Create your course

Add its name, dates and place in the app. Your course gets its own invite code, and you manage it as the owner.

Share one invite link

Send it on WhatsApp or show the QR code. It helps classmates install the app and join.

Approve and teach

Approve who joins, put the slides in the Library, and keep the class in one chat.

Safe by design: owners approve members, anyone can report misuse, and the community rules apply to everyone.

Questions

Is it free?

For students, yes: joining a course and your notebook are free. Trainers who want to run a course can write to support@liyaanote.app.

What does "beta" mean?

Liyaa Note is in its test phase with its first courses. Everything works, and we're improving it from your feedback. Tell us what you think at support@liyaanote.app.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. An iPhone version is on its way, through Apple's TestFlight first. For now, Liyaa Note is on Android.

Why does Android warn me when installing?

Liyaa Note is being tested on Google Play and isn't open to everyone there yet, so for now you install it from this website, and Android asks you to allow the install. The install guide shows exactly what to tap. Once it's on Google Play this step goes away.

Who can see my notes?

Only you. Your notebook stays on your phone, with an optional private backup. What you share in a course is seen only by that course's members. See the privacy policy.

What if I change phones?

Sign in on the new phone: your courses, chats and Memories are there, and your notes come back from your backup. Recordings and files can be moved with a backup file saved to your own Google Drive or OneDrive.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile & Settings → Delete my account. Or see how to delete your account.
