Inside a course

Every course you join has the same four parts. Your notebook is yours alone. The rest is shared with your class, and nobody outside it.

Notebook

Notes, photos, recordings, files and reminders, sorted by subject and topic. Private, and it works offline.

Library

Slides, handouts, videos and links for the whole class, by subject and topic, with search.

Chat

Everyone, small groups and private messages. Share a note or a file and @tag a classmate.

Memories

Class photos with comments and marks like Star, Proud and Legend. They stay after the course ends.

Running a course?

Liyaa Note is for institutions, coordinators and instructors. During the beta, we set up every course with you. Write to support@liyaanote.app with the course name, dates and place.

We set up the course

The Liyaa Note team creates it with you: name, dates and place. You become the owner, and the course gets its own invite code.

Share one link

Send the invite on WhatsApp or show its QR code. The link walks each person through installing the app and joining.

Approve who joins

Only people you approve get in. Anyone can report misuse, and the community rules apply to everyone.

Teach from the Library

Put the slides, handouts and video links in the Library. Members get a notification when new material arrives.

The class stays together

When the course ends, its chat and Memories stay open, so the class can keep in touch.

Questions

Is it free?

For students, yes: joining a course and your notebook are free. Courses are set up for institutions and instructors; during the beta, contact support@liyaanote.app.

What does “beta” mean?

Liyaa Note is in its test phase with its first courses. Everything works, and we’re improving it from your feedback. Tell us what you think at support@liyaanote.app.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Liyaa Note is Android-only for now. An iPhone version is planned.

Why does Android warn me when installing?

Liyaa Note isn’t on Google Play yet, so Android asks you to allow the install. The install guide shows exactly what to tap. Once it’s on Google Play this step goes away.

Who can see my notes?

Only you. Your notebook stays on your phone, with an optional private backup. What you share in a course is seen only by that course’s members. See the privacy policy.

What if I change phones?

Sign in on the new phone: your courses, chats and Memories are there, and your notes come back from your backup. Recordings and files can be moved with a backup file saved to your own Google Drive or OneDrive.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile & Settings, then Delete my account. Or see how to delete your account.
